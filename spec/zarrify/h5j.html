H5J¶

The Zarr layout of a Janelia H5J file (.h5j): an HDF5 file whose channels are each an MP4 file of monochrome HEVC, a picture a slice of a confocal stack, as Janelia’s FlyLight project and the Janelia Workstation write them and the Fiji H5J loader (JaneliaSciComp/H5J_Loader_Plugin) reads them. What all of refkv’s conventions share is in spec/conventions.md, cited here as “conventions §n”. How refkv produces this layout as a reference store, and which files it accepts, is the H5J profile, Part 2 below.

Convention version: 0 (until release, conventions §1) · UUID: 5bd31fac-d92d-452b-bcb6-15ca89c0f931 · Schema: schema.json

Changes: changes/h5j/, one file per change.

Added 2026-11-19.

This document has two parts (conventions §11.1).

Each channel is an OME-NGFF image of one level. Its pictures stay in the file as coded HEVC pictures; the array’s codec, refkv.stream (conventions §10), decodes them when a chunk is read. The HEVC stream is lossy against the microscope’s data: the arrays are the decoded stream, as every H5J reader gives it. The survey of public files and the design are in design/plans/h5j.md.

2. The source¶

An H5J file is an HDF5 file (hdf5.md reads it as one) with this layout, as the writers of the public files make it:

object

what it holds

the root group

the attributes channel_spec (a letter a channel: s signal, r reference), unit (micron), voxel_size (three numbers: x, y, z), image_size (three numbers: x, y, z), and others the writer adds (objective, area, owner, sample_id, alignment_space)

/Channels

a group, with the integer attributes width, height, frames, pad_right and pad_bottom, each an array of one element

/Channels/Channel_<n>

a channel: a contiguous dataset of one dimension of uint8, with the attribute content_type (signal or reference), whose bytes are a whole MP4 file (mp4.md) of one HEVC video track

The MP4 file’s offsets are from the start of the dataset’s bytes, so a sample at offset o of the MP4 file is at a + o of the H5J file, a the dataset’s address (with the base address, hdf5.md §8.2).

The pictures. The track’s pictures are 4:0:0 (monochrome) HEVC of 8, 10 or 12 bits; the public files’ are 12 bits, HEVC’s range extensions profile. Picture k in presentation order (mp4.md §4.1) is slice z = k, as the Fiji loader takes the decoder’s pictures in the order it outputs them.

The padding. An encoder codes a picture of a size that is a whole number of its blocks, so the writer pads each slice on the right and at the bottom, and records the padding in /Channels’s pad_right and pad_bottom. A slice is the coded picture, W by H (the sample entry’s width and height), less the padding: W − pad_right by H − pad_bottom, the columns and rows from the picture’s top left. This is the Fiji loader’s rule. The root’s image_size is not: in a public file it holds the coded size where /Channels’s width and height hold the slice’s (design/plans/h5j.md §1.4). Both are kept as source metadata (§5) and neither is read.

3. Data types¶

A slice’s samples are the decoder’s (conventions §10): uint8 for 8 bits, uint16 for 10 or 12, from 0 to 2^D − 1 (D the bit depth), not scaled.

4. The layout¶

The root is a group with no image of its own. Each channel the profile zarrifies (§8.2) is an image at its dataset’s name.

4.1 A channel¶

A channel of N pictures of W by H coded samples of D bits, with the padding pad_right and pad_bottom (each 0 when /Channels lacks it), is an image of one array, 0, its axes z, y and x.

The image. Its ome (OmeImage, OME-NGFF 0.5) has one multiscale: name the channel’s name, the axes z, y and x of type space, and one dataset, 0, of one scale transformation:

  • the scale is [v_z, v_y, v_x] when the root’s voxel_size is three numbers [v_x, v_y, v_z], each finite and greater than 0; else [1, 1, 1];

  • each axis has the unit of the root’s unit when it is one of micron, microns, micrometer, um, µm ("micrometer"), nanometer, nm ("nanometer"), millimeter or mm ("millimeter"); else no unit (conventions §5).

Its omero has one channel: label the dataset’s content_type attribute when it is text, else the channel’s name; color "FFFFFF"; active true; and the window 0 to 2^D − 1 shown whole.

5.3 Elements¶

The IR (conventions §8) is the HDF5 file’s IR (hdf5.md §7.3), but that the contiguous data of each channel the profile zarrifies is not one data element: it is a struct data of the same extent whose children are the elements of its MP4 file’s IR (mp4.md §5.3) but that file’s root, each extent moved a bytes on (a where the dataset’s bytes start). So the mirror holds the MP4 file’s boxes, and its samples are data elements, data/samples/<T>/<j> under the dataset’s object. A channel the profile doesn’t zarrify keeps its data element.

5.4 Equivalence¶

Two outputs are equivalent as spec/zarrify.md §1.1 says.

6. Example¶

The public file SS21347-20151117_32_D4-m-20x-brain-Split_GAL4-JRC2018_Unisex_20x_HR-aligned_stack.h5j of FlyLight’s Split-GAL4 Omnibus Broad release (7,666,989 bytes) has two channels of 174 pictures of 1216 × 568 coded samples of 12 bits, padded by 6 on the right and 2 at the bottom. Its entries, in part:

Channel_0/zarr.json           ome: axes z, y, x in micrometer, scale [1.0, 0.5189161, 0.5189161];

Channel_0/0/zarr.json         shape [174, 566, 1210], uint16, chunks [24, 568, 1216], refkv.stream hevc y

Channel_1/…                   "reference", the same shape
refkv_source/…                the mirror

7. What the layout does not express¶

This section is informative.

  • One array of channels. OME-NGFF wants an image’s channels on one axis of one array; a refkv.stream chunk decodes one stream into rows along its first axis, so each channel is an image of its own. The root lists them.

  • The other attributes’ meanings. channel_spec, image_size, objective, alignment_space and the rest are recorded, not interpreted.

  • The time stamps. The pictures’ presentation times order the slices and mean nothing more; they are in the mirror, and no array holds them.

  • Pictures of chroma. A channel of 4:2:0 or 4:2:2 pictures, which no public file has, is not zarrified (§8.2).

Part 2. The profile¶

8. H5J profile¶

The profile reads the file as the HDF5 profile does (hdf5.md §8): every check of that profile rejects an H5J file too. Then it reads each channel’s MP4 file as the MP4 profile does (mp4.md §8), each read moved by the dataset’s address.

8.1 Choosing the profile¶

The profile has no signature. The HDF5 profile chooses it (hdf5.md §8.1; its registry entry’s chosen_by is hdf5): an HDF5 file that is not an Imaris file, whose root group has a link Channels (hard, or soft through hard links) to a group with at least one link, every one of them named Channel_<n> (n one to nine decimal digits), is taken for an H5J file. A structure on that path that can’t be read makes the file no H5J file. The names are generic, so the channels confirm the choice: a file taken for an H5J file that fails one of the checks of §8.3, as one with no channel to present does, is the HDF5 profile’s, whose output it then has. A file named as the H5J profile’s input (spec/zarrify.md §1.7) that is not an H5J file is rejected: not an H5J file: the root has no group Channels of links Channel_<n>.

The profile reads one file. The files an H5J file names, by external links or external storage (hdf5.md §8.6), are not read: the HDF5 profile’s rules for a file whose siblings are not found apply. A store run of the HDF5 profile reads every file it reads as HDF5, an H5J file among them.

8.2 Channels¶

A channel is each object /Channels/Channel_<n>, in order of n. It is zarrified unless one of these holds, checked in this order; the first that holds is its reason (§5.1), and the channel keeps its dataset’s data element (§5.3):

  • it is no dataset: not a dataset of bytes;

  • its data type is not uint8, or it has other than one dimension;

  • its storage is not contiguous (compact, chunked, external or not allocated);

  • the MP4 profile rejects its bytes, read as a file: its MP4 file: and the rejection;

  • the MP4 file has other than one track;

  • the MP4 profile doesn’t zarrify the track (mp4.md §8.2): its reason;

  • the track is not H.264, HEVC, AV1 or VP9 video;

  • its pictures are not 4:0:0;

  • /Channels’s pad_right or pad_bottom is not one integer of at least 0;

  • pad_right is not less than W, or pad_bottom not less than H.

8.3 Checks¶

In this order, after the HDF5 profile’s. A file the HDF5 profile took for an H5J file (§8.1) that fails one is the HDF5 profile’s instead; a file named as the H5J profile’s input is rejected with the message.

  1. Channels: at most 1024: budget: more than 1024 channels.

  2. A channel: the profile zarrifies at least one (§8.2): the message names each channel’s reason, joined by ; : no channel can be zarrified: Channel_0: its pictures are 4:2:0, not 4:0:0 (monochrome).

8.4 Chunks¶

A channel’s chunks are those of the MP4 profile’s y array of its track (mp4.md §4.1, §8.3): at most F = min(256, max(1, floor(2^25 / (W × H × B)))) pictures (B the bytes of a sample), cut at the sync samples’ presentation indexes, each chunk the refkv.stream chunk of its pictures and their lead-in, its frames ranges of the H5J file. The public files have one or two sync samples, so a chunk past the first decodes from picture 0.
