# QPTIFF: stored arrays

What the QPTIFF parser describes as stored arrays ([stored-array.md](../../stored-array.md)), part of [the QPTIFF profile](../qptiff.md).

A QPTIFF is read as a TIFF, and its
IFDs' images are described as a TIFF's, by the same code; its facts' levels give `declared_by`
likewise.
